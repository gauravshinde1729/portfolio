title: "Decentralized Transaction System for Detection and Prevention of Fraud in Crowdfunding Platforms"
abstract: 'Crowdfunding via the Internet is a new channel for raising money for startups. It features low barriers to entry, low cost, and high speed, and thus encourages innovation. However, people/entrepreneurs return no value to the contributors after money is raised. This paper focuses on creating a decentralized system using blockchain technologies that will detect and prevent fraud happening in crowdfunding and also be able to provide value back to the contributors either in the form of assets or trust. Blockchain is a decentralized and distributed ledger technology to ensure data security, transparency, and integrity. Because it cannot be tampered with or forged, the technology is deemed to have great potential in the finance industry. This paper examines current problems in the practice of crowd-funding and aims to build a "Decentralized Transaction System" that helps in the detection and prevention of fraud in crowdfunding platforms. The contributors to the campaign will no longer be sending the money directly to the "Idea Person". Instead, they will be sending the money to the Ethereum Contract. This Ethereum contract will have the logic that regulates who can receive the money from the campaign. Here, the "Idea Person" will create the "Spending Request". This would withdraw money from the contract and send it directly to the vendor. Also, already existing contributors have the right to vote (approve) on every "Spending Request". If enough people approve the "Spending Request", the funds will automatically be sent to the vendor. This would eventually allow us to reduce the number of frauds that are being done on crowdfunding platforms.'
